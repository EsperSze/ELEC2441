Relative dynamic power

Suppose a new processor Y is developed with 84% of the capacitive load of a previous processor X. In addition, both the frequency and voltage of Y are reduced by 12% when compared to X. Which of the following statements is correct?

  1. The dynamic power of X is 0.552 of the dynamic power of Y.
  2. The dynamic power of Y is 0.552 of the dynamic power of X.
  3. The dynamic power of Y is 0.572 of the dynamic power of X.
  4. The dynamic power of Y is 0.592 of the dynamic power of X.
  5. The dynamic power of Y is 0.621 of the dynamic power of X.
Answer & explanation

Correct answer: C. The dynamic power of Y is approximately 0.572 of the dynamic power of X.

Use the lecture formula:

\[\text{Dynamic power}=\text{Capacitance}\times(\text{CPU voltage})^2\times\text{Clock rate}\]

A 12% reduction leaves $1-0.12=0.88$ of the original value. The capacitance factor is 0.84, while both the voltage and frequency factors are 0.88.

\[\frac{\text{Dynamic power of Y}}{\text{Dynamic power of X}}=0.84\times(0.88)^2\times0.88\]
\[=0.57243648\approx0.572\]

Y therefore uses about 57.2% of X's dynamic power, a reduction of about 42.8%.

Common pitfalls: square the voltage factor; use 0.88 rather than 0.12 for the reduced values; keep the ratio in the order Y divided by X.
